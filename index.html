    How this is calculated
    • Hours saved = people × hours a week on AI-suited tasks × how much faster AI makes them × 4.345 weeks a month.
    • Value of time saved = hours saved × cost of one hour. This is time freed up, not cash saved: it becomes value when the team spends it on other work.
    • Monthly AI cost, per-seat subscription = people × the plan's price per seat, plus other monthly costs.
    • Monthly AI cost, pay as you go = for each kind of work ticked, people × requests a week × 4.345 × (tokens sent × input price + tokens received × output price), plus other monthly costs. The usage behind each kind of work is an estimate; tick "enter figures myself" to use your own.
    • Benefit after AI costs = value of time saved − monthly AI cost.

    It leaves out one-off costs such as rollout and training, and the time it takes a team to get up to speed.
